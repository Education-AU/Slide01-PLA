Such representing vectors $\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2$ are called 
**basis vectors** 

Given any such **choice** of basis vectors, we can represent any vector as a **unique** 
pair of coordinates namely the scalars that are multiplied on 
the basis vectors in the representation.



Choosing the basis

$$
\mathbf{b}_1=\mathbf{(1,0)}, \quad \mathbf{b}_2=\mathbf{(0,1)}
$$

which is called the **standard basis**


The coordinates for any vector $\mathbf{(x_1,x_2)}$ are as the equation from before showed

$$
(x_1, x_2)
$$
