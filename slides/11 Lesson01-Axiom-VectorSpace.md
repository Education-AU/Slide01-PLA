A vector space over a field $\mathbb{F}$ is a **set** $V$ equipped with two operations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\oplus &: V \times V \rightarrow V \quad \text{ (vector addition)}\\
\odot &: \mathbb{F} \times V \rightarrow V \quad \text{ (scalar multiplication)}
\end{aligned}
$$

<div class ="center">
These operations must satisfy a collection of 8 axioms.
</div>

<br><br>
Notice we do not simply write $+$ and $\cdot$ for these operations.

This distinction is important: the operations $\oplus$ and $\odot$
are **not** the usual addition and multiplication
on the field ($\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$).
They are new operations defined on the set $V$, even though they
are designed to resemble the familiar ones.

Later, once the context is clear, we will drop this notation and use $+$ and $\cdot$. 

For now, keeping them separate helps avoid confusion.
