We have consistently distinguished between $\oplus,\odot$ and $+,\cdot$

But from now on we lighten the notation by dropping this 
more correct but somewhat verbose notation and just 
use $+$ and $\cdot$ or maybe not even the $\cdot$, but 
just assume multiplication if nothing points against it. 

So, we will **knowingly** identify the following expressions.
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{v}_1 \oplus \mathbf{v}_2&= \mathbf{v}_1+\mathbf{v}_2\\
s \odot \mathbf{v}&= s\cdot \mathbf{v}=s\mathbf{v}
\end{aligned}
$$
In many cases for clarity we will boldface the vectors to distinguish scalars from vectors
