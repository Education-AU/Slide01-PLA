Many important examples of vector spaces are defined over

1. The field of real numbers $\mathbb{R}$
2. The field of complex numbers $\mathbb{C}$

which will be the main focus in this course.

And we will eventually focus on so-called **real** vector spaces over the field $\mathbb{R}$.

But for now

To avoid repeatedly writing $\mathbb{R}$ or $\mathbb{C}$, we denote the
underlying field by $\mathbb{F}$, which can be understood as one of these fields unless stated otherwise.
