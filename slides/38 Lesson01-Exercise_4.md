Consider the vector space $\mathbb{R}^2$ and consider the basis vectors
$$
\mathbf{b}_1= (7,3), \quad \mathbf{b}_1= (-1,4)
$$

Determine the coordinates for vectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,2)$ in this basis

**Hint:**
To determine any vector $(a,b)$ coordinates in the basis you must solve the linear system
$$
\begin{aligned}
c_1\cdot \mathbf{b}_1+c_2\cdot\mathbf{b}_2= (a,b)\\
c_1\cdot (7,3)+c_2\cdot (-1,4)= (a,b)
\end{aligned}
$$
which expanded in two equations is

$$
\begin{aligned}
7c_1-c_2=a\\
3c_1+4c_2=b
\end{aligned}
$$
