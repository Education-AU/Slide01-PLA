5. **Associative law for scalar multiplication:**<br>
For all $s_1,s_2 \in \mathbb{F}$ and all $\mathbf{v}\in V$<br>
$(s_1\cdot s_2)\odot \mathbf{v} =s_1\odot (s_2 \odot \mathbf{v})$

6. **Law of Distribution of scalar multiplication over vector addition:**<br>
For all $\mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2 \in V$, and all $s\in \mathbb{F}$<br>
$s\odot (\mathbf{v}_1 \oplus \mathbf{v}_2)= (s\odot \mathbf{v}_1 \oplus s\odot \mathbf{v}_2)$

7. **Law of Distribution of scalar multiplication over scalar addition:**<br>
For all $\mathbf{v} \in V$, and all $s_1, s_2\in \mathbb{F}$
$(s_1+s_2)\odot \mathbf{v}= (s_1\odot \mathbf{v} \oplus s_2\odot \mathbf{v})$

8. **Law of existence of scalar multiplicative identity:**<br>
For all $\mathbf{v}\in V$ the multiplicative identity $1$ with respect to multiplication in $\mathbb{F}$ has the property
$1\odot \mathbf{v}= \mathbf{v}$
