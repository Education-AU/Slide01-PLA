This vector space is the focus of the associated book and has a lot of 
applications. It is also the vector space most familiar to you.

The basic set is $\mathbb{R}^n$. That is the set of all ordered n-tuples of real numbers.

$$
\mathbb{R}^n=\{ (x_1,x_2,...,x_n)|x_i \in \mathbb{R}\}
$$

The associated field is $\mathbb{R}$

And the operations are defined by
$$
\begin{aligned}
(x_1,x_2,...,x_n)\oplus (y_1,y_2,...,y_n)&=
(x_1+y_1,x_2+y_2,...,x_n+y_n)\\
s\odot (x_1,x_2,...,x_n)&= (s\cdot x_1,s \cdot x_2,...,s\cdot x_n)
\end{aligned}
$$
The additive identity is and inverse are defined as

$\textbf{0}=(0,0,...,0)$ and $-(x_1,x_2,...,x_n)=(-x_1,-x_2,...,-x_n)$

From this, all the axioms will follow, which is also left as an exercise.
