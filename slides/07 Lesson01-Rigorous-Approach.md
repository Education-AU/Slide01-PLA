### Style of lesson
We begin with a rigorous mathematical presentation to be precise in our definitions of the basic concepts of linear algebra, 
before quickly specializing to the case most relevant for this course.

### Reasoning
Introducing the general theory is essential: without it, understanding other applications of linear algebra would be difficult.

### First Step
Since linear algebra is fundamentally about vector spaces and linear structures on such spaces, 
we start by defining the concept of a **Vector Space** axiomatically.
