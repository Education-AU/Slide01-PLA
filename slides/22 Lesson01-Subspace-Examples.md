Consider $\mathbb{P}^n$ the vector space of all polynomials of max degree $n$

Let $U$ be all polynomials that have zeroes at say $x_1, x_2$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $U$ be all polynomials with $maximum\leq M$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $U\subset \mathbb{R}^n =\{ (a,b,0,0,\dots,0)|a,b\in \mathbb{R}\}$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $n=2$ and $U=\{ (x,y)| y=ax | a\in \mathbb{R}\}$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $n=2$ and $U=\{ (x,y)| y=ax+b | a,b\in \mathbb{R}, b\neq 0\}$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $n=3$ and $U=\{(x,y,z)| x+y-z=0 \}$. Is $U$ a subspace?

Let $n=3$ and $U=\{(x,y,z)| x+y-z=1 \}$. Is $U$ a subspace?
