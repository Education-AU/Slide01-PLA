Is this representation unique??

Let $v\in \mathbb{R}^2$

And assume that $v$ has two different representations
$$
\begin{aligned}
v=c_1\mathbf{b}_1+c_2\mathbf{b}_2\\
v=d_1\mathbf{b}_1+d_2\mathbf{b}_2
\end{aligned}
$$

Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
(c_1-d_1)\mathbf{b}_1+(c_2-d_2)\mathbf{b}_2=\mathbf{0}\\
\end{aligned}
$$

If the vectors $\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2$ have the property that

$$
x_1\mathbf{b}_1+x_2\mathbf{b}_2=\mathbf{0} \Rightarrow x_1=x_2=0
$$

Then

$$
c1=d1,\quad c_2=d_2
$$
