Make a drawing on a piece of paper representing the vectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,2)$ by coordinates in the basis
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{b}_1&=(1,1)\\
\mathbf{b}_2&=(-1,1)
\end{aligned}
$$

and in the standard basis
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{e}_1= (1,0)\\
\mathbf{e}_2= (0,1)
\end{aligned}
$$

Add to the drawing the sum of the vector coordinates of vectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,2)$ in both basis
