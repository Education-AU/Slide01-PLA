A vector space over the field $\mathbb{C}$ is called a **Complex Vector Space**

A vector space over the field $\mathbb{R}$ is called a **Real Vector Space**

</div>
<br><br>

Having stated the axioms we now turn to examples of vector spaces to get some feeling  of the concept.

In the end we will focus on a very special **real** 
vector space called $\mathbb{R}^n$ which might be the vector 
space most familiar to you.
