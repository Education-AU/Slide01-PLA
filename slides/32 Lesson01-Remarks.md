This concept of basis is essential in vector spaces and the fact that many such basis exists is very important, because any coordinate representation of a vector in a vector space must state which basis the representation is given in.



We will come back to basis in a more general setting and state the concept in a precise manner. But for now we continue with this observation in mind.



**Remark:**

The boldface notation of vectors $\mathbf{(0,1)}$ will be omitted from now on unless to stress the difference between coordinates and vectors.
