The course combines two perspectives: understanding linear algebra as a mathematical subject
and applying it to practical problems using various libraries such as *NumPy*.

While the book emphasizes practice, the course also introduces some underlying mathematical rigor.

The course focus is:

<div class="center dash-list">
- Rudimentary mathematical foundations <br>
- Practical applications<br>
- Computation in Python with NumPy and other libraries<br>
</div>
