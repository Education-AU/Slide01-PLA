So in coordinates the sum of the vectors $(v_1,v_2)$ and $(w_1,w_2)$ is
$$
(v_1+w_1,v_2+w_2)
$$
and in coordinates the multiplication with a scalar in coordinates is
$$
(sv_1,sv_2)
$$

and the calculated coordinates translates back to vectors by applying them to the basis vectors

Mind you, this principle holds in **any** vector space not just $\mathbb{R}^2$
