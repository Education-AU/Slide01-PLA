We check uniqueness of previous representation

$$
c_1\mathbf{(1,0)}+c_2\mathbf{(0,1)}=\mathbf{(0,0)}
$$

implies

$$
\mathbf{(c_1,c_2)}=\mathbf{(0,0)}
$$

which again implies $c_1=c_2=0$

This property of the two vectors $\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2$ is called **linear independence**, and it will be an important concept.
