Consider the vector space $\mathbb{R}^3$

Consider the vectors:

$\mathbf{b}_1=(7,3,1), \quad \mathbf{b}_1=(-13,2,-17)$

Define the subset $U$ by: 

$U=\{ a\cdot \mathbf{b}_1+b\cdot  \mathbf{b}_2|a,b\in \mathbb{R}\}$


1. Is $U$ a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$?

2. Does this hold for any two vectors $\mathbf{b}_1,\mathbf{b}_2$?
