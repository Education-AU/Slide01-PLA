1. **Commutative law for addition:**<br>
   For all $\mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2 \in V$, $\mathbf{v}_1\oplus \mathbf{v}_2=\mathbf{v}_2\oplus \mathbf{v}_1$

2. **Associative law for addition:**<br>
   For
   all $\mathbf{v}_1,\mathbf{v}_2,\mathbf{v}_3 \in V$, $(\mathbf{v}_1\oplus \mathbf{v}_2)\oplus \mathbf{v}_3=\mathbf{v}_1\oplus (\mathbf{v}_2\oplus \mathbf{v}_3)$

3. **Law of existence of additive identity:**<br>
   There exists an element $\mathbf{0} \in V $ such that for all $\mathbf{v}\in V , \mathbf{v} \oplus \mathbf{0}=\mathbf{v}$<br>
   $\mathbf{0}$ is just a familiar name for this special element

4. **Law of existence of inverse element for addition:**<br>
For all elements $\mathbf{v}\in V $ there exists an element $-\mathbf{v}$ such that<br>
$\mathbf{v}\oplus -\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{0}$
