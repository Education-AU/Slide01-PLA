We first restrict our attention to the vector space $(\mathbb{R}^2,+,\cdot)$.

All vectors in $(\mathbb{R}^2,+,\cdot)$ can be represented as

$\mathbf{(x_1,x_2)}= x_1\cdot \mathbf{(1,0)}+ x_2\cdot\mathbf{(0,1)}$

Which is simply checked by the definition

$x_1\cdot \mathbf{(1,0)}+ x_2\cdot \mathbf{(0,1)}=\mathbf{(x_1,0)} + \mathbf{(0,x_2)}=\mathbf{(x_1,x_2)}$

We say that any vector can be expressed by a linear combination of the two explicit vectors

$\mathbf{b}_1=\mathbf{(1,0)}, \quad \mathbf{b}_2=\mathbf{(0,1)}$
